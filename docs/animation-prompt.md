# Terra Vox: animation prompt and narrator script (30 seconds)

For an optional AI-animated version of the demo. The live-footage video in `demo/terra-vox-demo-30s.mp4` is already submission-ready; this is a more cinematic alternative or an add-on for the hook.

## Rules that decide how to use it

- **30 seconds or less in total** (the demo limit for Global Awards).
- The NASA form says AI-generated images and videos **must carry a visible watermark saying they are AI-generated**. Put a small "AI-generated animation" tag in a corner of every AI shot. Real screen recordings of the app need no tag.
- No NASA logo, meatball, worm or flag anywhere (the form forbids generated content that uses NASA branding).
- AI video tools draw garbled text and invent data. Generate **pictures only, no text, no numbers**. Add every word and number yourself in the editor, from the real app.
- Keep the real app in the film. Cut between the AI hero shots and 2 to 3 real recordings, so judges see that the sound and data are real.

## Why people watch to the end (what this script does)

1. **Sound in the first second.** No logo, no title: the first thing is a low note in the left ear.
2. **An open question:** "hear the gap". The answer comes at second 9, then a new question comes right after.
3. **A change every 3 seconds:** a new picture, a new number, or a new sound.
4. **Two surprises:** the sea level bass climbing an octave, and the line about the people who cannot see a chart.
5. **A last line that finishes the story:** it returns to "close your eyes" from the start.

## Narrator script (about 62 words, 28 seconds, leaves room for the music)

Speak slowly and quietly, like telling a secret. Best voice: **your own, recorded on a phone in a quiet room**. It is free, it sounds human, and it avoids the AI-voice disclosure. If you use an AI voice instead, the credit line must stay in the video and its description.

| Time | Narration | What is on screen | What is heard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | "Close your eyes. This is Earth in 2003." | Black, then the left half of a globe fades in, cool blue and green | One low, calm note, left ear only |
| 0:03-0:06 | "Now. This is 2026." | The right half lights up, amber and red | A higher, busier melody, right ear only |
| 0:06-0:10 | "Same planet. One year in each ear. That gap you hear? Twenty-three years of methane." | Both halves together; rings ripple out where the satellite measured; counter 1,756 to 1,881 ppb | Both ears at once |
| 0:10-0:15 | "Terra Vox turns real NASA satellite data into music. More methane, higher and busier." | Colours of a NASA map turning into notes (AI shot), then the real app | Melody rises |
| 0:15-0:19 | "Sea level rose seven point six centimetres. Hear the bass climb." | Ocean globe; a low note rising an octave | Bass climbs E-flat 3 to E-flat 4 |
| 0:19-0:23 | "And it matches NOAA's own records." | Real app: the 0.992 and 0.997 proof band | Music steady, quieter |
| 0:23-0:27 | "Three hundred thirty-eight million people can't read a chart. Now they can hear one." | A person with headphones, eyes closed (AI shot) | Music swells softly |
| 0:27-0:30 | "Terra Vox. Press play." | Globe, the web address, credits | Last chord, silence |

Facts the script relies on (all from the app): 2003 left, 2026 right; methane at 400 hPa 1,756 to 1,881 ppb, January to September of both years; sea level 1993 to 2018, -3.4 to +4.2 cm; r = 0.992 and 0.997 against NOAA; the bass climbs E-flat 3 to E-flat 4; 338 million is the count of people who are blind or have moderate to severe vision loss (GBD 2019).

## Master prompt (paste into a text-to-video tool)

> Cinematic, quiet, intimate. A slowly rotating planet Earth floats in a black void, split down the middle by a thin dark seam. The left half is cool: soft teal and pale green, calm and still, with a faint blue glow on its rim. The right half is warm: deep amber and red, restless, with a faint gold glow on its rim. Thin glowing rings ripple outward from points on the surface, like drops of light falling on water, each ring brighter on the right side than the left. Tiny soft light particles drift like sound waves. Shallow depth of field, smooth slow dolly-in, soft film grain, deep blacks, high contrast, 16:9, 24 fps. Style: premium science documentary meets abstract data art. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no flags, no people's faces, no spacecraft.

**Negative prompt:** text, letters, numbers, watermark, logo, NASA logo, flag, cartoon, low quality, fast shaky camera, lens flare overload, extra moons, human faces.

## Shot list (make each as its own 5-8 second clip, then cut to 2-3 s each in an editor)

1. **Left half only.** "A planet seen from space, only its left half is lit, cool teal and pale green, one soft ring of light ripples slowly across the surface, black void, calm, still camera." Tag: AI-generated.
2. **Right half only.** "The same planet, only its right half is lit, warm amber and red, four quick rings of golden light ripple across the surface, restless energy." Tag: AI-generated.
3. **Both together.** "The same planet, split by a thin dark seam, left half cool teal, right half warm amber, rings of light ripple on both sides, slow push-in." Tag: AI-generated.
4. **Colours become music.** "Macro shot of a satellite image of colour bands (green to yellow to red) lifting off the surface and turning into glowing sound-wave ribbons that rise into the dark." No text. Tag: AI-generated.
5. **Ocean.** "Dark ocean planet, low deep waves of light slowly climbing upward like a rising bass note, blue and white, calm." Tag: AI-generated.
6. **Listener.** "Close-up of a person with their eyes gently closed wearing over-ear headphones, soft blue light on the left side of their face and soft amber light on the right, shallow depth of field, peaceful." (A generic, non-identifiable person; no real or named person.) Tag: AI-generated.

Cut order: 1, 2, 3, real app (Duet), 4, real app (methane counter), 5, real app (proof band), 6, end card.

- Captions burned in for every spoken line (so it works muted and for deaf viewers).
- A visible "AI-generated" tag on each AI shot; the end card says who made it and how: "Built with Claude Code. AI-generated animation shots." and, if used, "Narration: AI voice (ElevenLabs)."
